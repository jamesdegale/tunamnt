House Notes

🏠Home Base

Aaron’s cabin — 13454 Island View Rd, Crosslake, MN 56442. About 2½ hours northwest of MSP. Roughly 20 minutes to The Preserve in Pequot Lakes, about 30 to The Pines in Nisswa.

On the water, so the boat runs to dinner straight off the dock.

⛳The U.S. Open

Playing out at Shinnecock Hills, Southampton NY — Thu–Sun, the same four days as the TunaMNt.

Each night ends with golf at the cabin: the day’s replay or Live From on Golf Channel. Sat & Sun we catch it live.

🏌️The Format

Not every-man-for-himself. Pick teams at the first tee each day and play four-ball (better ball) — two-man sides, low score of the pair counts.

Partners rotate, so over four rounds everyone’s teamed with everyone at least once.

⚽England v. Croatia

World Cup Group L opener, kickoff 3:00 PM Central on Wed the 17th (9 PM UK), from AT&T Stadium in Arlington.

Scotty’s non-negotiable — he follows soccer once every four years, and only while England are any good. They’re good right now. A watch-it-out game; figure roughly 3 to 5 PM.

🛥️Lunch by Boat

Post-round Thu & Fri, ride Aaron’s boat to a long lunch with golf on the TVs. Zorbaz and The Wharf both sit on Cross Lake with dock access — pull right up, then retreat before it gets late.

🍳The Galley — Aaron

Dinners are at the cabin and Aaron’s got the whole operation: cooking and, by group decree, the cleanup.

Which means no “DisAstrom” from Chris this year — the kitchen’s closed to him on Aaron’s watch.

🪰Beware the Horseflies

Per Aaron, the state bird of Minnesota is the mosquito — those you can fight off. The horseflies are a different sitch.

Bug spray, sleeves, accept your fate near the water, and swing through it.

👕The Kit

Everyone packs at least one British Open outfit — long sleeves and pants. Less about the temperature, more as armor against everything flying around up there.

Bring rain wear, and pack for layering — not something you’d bother with down south. It should run warm to hot, but nobody up there ever really knows, and layers give you options.

And bathing suits, of course — the cabin’s on the water. It all counts toward Best Dressed, naturally.

Four rounds, one boat, and the U.S. Open on in the background.
